# Medical history photographs

Images were resized and converted to WebP. The listed source pages provide the original files and their licensing information.

- **fleming.webp** — [Alexander Fleming in his laboratory](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Synthetic_Production_of_Penicillin_TR1468.jpg), official photographer, 1943. Public domain, UK Government photograph ([PD-UKGov](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-UKGov)).
- **barnard.webp** — [Christiaan Barnard](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Heart_transplant_pioneer_Barnard_here_on_Visit_(FL61733185).jpg), IPPA photographer, Dan Hadani collection, National Library of Israel, 1972. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **hpylori.webp** — [Helicobacter pylori electron micrograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EMpylori.jpg), Yutaka Tsutsumi, M.D. Copyrighted free use, as stated on the source page.
- **pasteur.webp** — [Louis Pasteur](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Louis_Pasteur,_foto_av_Paul_Nadar,_Crisco_edit.jpg), photograph by Paul Nadar, edited by Crisco 1492. Public domain ([Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/)).
- **koch.webp** — [Robert Koch](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RobertKoch_cropped.jpg), photographer unknown, US National Institutes of Health. Public domain ([PD-USGov-NIH](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-USGov-NIH)).
